ABOUT US

Two surfers. A lot of memory cards.

Surfers of India films and photographs surf sessions along the Indian coast — the first paddle-out, the big wipeout, the ride back in. We shoot the session, then use this site so you can find your own moments with a selfie instead of scrolling through hundreds of photos of strangers.

How it works

Pick your session, upload a clear selfie, and we match it against the photos from that day. Watermarked previews are free to browse. If we found you, you can unlock the full-resolution, watermark-free set for a small fee, paid securely through Cashfree.

The legal bit

Surfers of India is an unregistered sole proprietorship operated by Ankit Kotian, based in India. Payments for photo packs are collected on behalf of Surfers of India and processed by Cashfree Payments; we never see or store your card details.

Get in touch

Questions about a session, a payment, or a match that doesn't look right? Email namaste@surfersofindia.com or find us on Instagram @surfersofindia.
