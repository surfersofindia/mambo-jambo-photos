A NOTE ON YOUR PRIVACY

A little trust goes a long way.

With your consent, your selfie is uploaded to our API and forwarded to the face-processing service. The app uses the resulting face data to compare against your selected session. The selfie and its face data are not saved by this app.

Session photos and their face data are stored for matching. A search record containing matched photo IDs is created, and preview access is temporary. Matching can make mistakes. Only upload your own selfie; contact the surf school crew for help with incorrect matches or data requests.
